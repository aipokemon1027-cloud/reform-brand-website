Our Story
我們的故事

Precision-engineered for women who demand more from their practice.
為追求更高品質練習的女性而設計。

We believe that every detail matters.
Every thread. Every grip. Every breath.
我們相信每個細節都很重要。

reform. was founded in Taipei by a team of Pilates instructors and product designers who shared one frustration: the socks available on the market simply weren't good enough. They slipped, bunched, and distracted from what should be a focused, meditative practice. reform. 由一群 Pilates 教練和產品設計師在台北創立，我們有一個共同的挫折：市場上現有的襪子根本不夠好。它們會滑落、捲曲，讓應該專注的練習分心。

So we made our own. After two years of research, prototyping, and testing with over 500 practitioners, we created socks that stay in place, feel incredible, and last. 所以我們自己製作。經過兩年的研究、開發和超過 500 位練習者的測試，我們創造了能固定位置、舒適感驚人且耐用的襪子。

Designed in Taipei, Crafted for the World
台北設計，服務全球

Every pair of reform. socks goes through 12 quality checkpoints before reaching you.
每一雙 reform. 襪子在到達您手中前都會經過 12 道品質檢查。
