Return Policy
退貨政策

30-Day Returns 30天退貨

We offer a 30-day return policy for unworn and unwashed items in their original packaging. Simply contact us to initiate a return. 我們為未穿著、未洗滌且保持原包裝的商品提供 30 天退貨政策。

How to Return 如何退貨

  1. Contact us via email at hello@reform-pilates.com 透過電子郵件聯繫我們
  2. Receive a return authorization number 取得退貨授權號碼
  3. Ship the item back in original packaging 使用原包裝寄回商品
  4. Refunds processed within 5-7 business days 退款將在 5-7 個工作天內處理

Exchanges 換貨

Need a different size or color? We offer free exchanges within 30 days. Just let us know what you'd like to change. 需要不同的尺寸或顏色？我們在 30 天內提供免費換貨服務。
