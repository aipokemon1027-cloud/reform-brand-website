Materials that matter 素材的故事

Organic combed cotton, recycled nylon grip zones, and a cuff that holds without binding. Each sock is assembled with flat-seam construction to eliminate friction points — because the only thing you should feel is your practice. 有機精梳棉、再生尼龍防滑區域，以及不緊勒的襪口。每一雙襪子均採用平縫工藝，消除摩擦點——你只需專注於練習，別的交給 reform。

Anti-slip Grip 防滑抓地

Silicone grip zones at the heel and ball of the foot. Engineered to hold on wood, cork, and sticky mats alike. 後跟與腳掌部位的矽膠防滑區域，適用於木地板、軟木墊與黏性瑜伽墊。

Arch Support 弓形支撐

A built-in arch band that hugs the midfoot without squeezing. Added stability for every balance pose. 內建弓形支撐帶，貼合足弓而不緊勒，為所有平衡體式提供穩定支撐。

Breathable Mesh 透氣網眼

Open-knit mesh across the top of the foot lets air circulate. No clammy studio sessions. 腳背透氣網眼設計，加速空氣流通。長時間練習也能保持乾爽。
