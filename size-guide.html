How to Measure 如何測量

  1. Stand on a piece of paper with your heel against a wall 您的腳跟靠牆站在紙上
  2. Trace the outline of your foot 描繪您的腳輪廓
  3. Measure from heel to longest toe 測量從腳跟到最長腳趾的距離
  4. Compare to our size chart above 與上面的尺寸表比較
