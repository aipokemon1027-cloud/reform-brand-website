品牌故事Brand Story

我們的故事Our Story

reform. 的起點，是一個關於「足夠」的問題。我們相信，真正好的設計，是不需要解釋的。 reform. began with a question about "enough." We believe the best design needs no explanation.

起點The Beginning

一切始於一個簡單的問題 We started with a simple question

我們問自己：什麼是真正需要的？什麼只是想要的？這兩者之間，有沒有一個安靜的空間？reform. 就是那個空間。 We asked ourselves: what do we truly need, and what do we merely want? Is there a quiet space between them? That's where reform. lives.

理念Philosophy

reform. 的理念 The reform. way

安靜不是沉默。安靜是一種選擇。當世界越來越嘈雜，我們選擇精挑細選。每一件作品，都是我們對「少而更好」的反覆思考。 Quiet is not silence. Quiet is a choice. As the world grows louder, we choose carefully. Each piece is our meditation on "less but better."

核心價值Core Values

我們相信的事 What we believe

◐

沉靜的自信 Quiet Confidence

真正的力量，不需要證明。我們的設計，是留給知道自己要什麼的人。 Real strength needs no proof. Our design is for those who know what they want.

◎

有目的的設計 Purposeful Design

每一個細節都有理由。沒有裝飾，只有選擇。 Every detail has a reason. No decoration, only intention.

◑

用心的細節 Intentional Details

從針腳到布料，從版型到包裝，我們在乎那些看不見的部分。 From stitching to fabric, from cut to packaging — we care about what you don't see.

創辦人的話Founder's Note

「我不想要時尚。我想要的是一個可以穿十年的東西，一個在雜亂中讓人冷靜下來的東西，一個不需要Logo就能說話的東西。reform. 就是這樣開始的。」 "I didn't want fashion. I wanted something I could wear for ten years. Something calming in the noise. Something that speaks without a logo. That's how reform. began."

— 創辦人— The Founder
