FAQ
常見問題

Find answers to common questions about reform. products.
找到關於 reform. 產品的常見問題答案。

What size should I order? 我應該訂購什麼尺寸？

Our socks come in S, M, and L. Check our Size Guide 尺寸指南 for detailed measurements. If you're between sizes, we recommend sizing up for comfort.

How do I care for my reform. socks? 如何保養我的 reform. 襪子？

Machine wash cold with like colors. Tumble dry low. Avoid bleach and ironing to maintain grip quality. For best results, wash inside out. 冷水機洗，分色洗滌。低溫烘乾。避免漂白和熨燙以保持防滑品質。

Can I use reform. socks for other activities? 我可以將 reform. 襪子用於其他活動嗎？

While designed for Pilates, our socks are great for yoga, barre, dance, and any activity where you need grip and stability. 雖然為 Pilates 設計，我們的襪子也適合瑜伽、芭蕾、舞蹈和任何需要防滑和穩定性的活動。

Do you offer international shipping? 你們提供國際配送嗎？

Yes! We ship worldwide. Check our Shipping 配送 page for details and rates.

What if I'm not satisfied with my purchase? 如果我對購買不滿意怎麼辦？

We offer a 30-day return policy. If you're not completely satisfied, contact us for a full refund or exchange. See our Returns 退換 page for details.

Still have questions? 還有問題嗎？

Contact us 聯繫我們 and we'll be happy to help!
