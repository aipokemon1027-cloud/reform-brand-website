不只是襪子：reform. 的誕生故事

More Than Socks: The Birth of reform.

從第一雙樣品到現在的產品，這是我們三年來的故事。

From the first prototype to the product on your feet — three years of iteration, failure, and breakthrough.

那個下午，在一張普通的墊子上

That Afternoon, on an Ordinary Mat

二〇二一年秋天。我第一次接觸墊上運動。不是在健身房，是在一個朋友家裡的客廳。她剛從一趟很長的旅程回來，說身體感覺不對，想要動一下。我就坐在旁邊，看著她。

Autumn 2021. I first encountered mat work. Not at a gym—in a friend's living room. She'd just returned from a long trip and said her body felt off, that she wanted to move. I sat beside her and watched.

她的動作很慢，很控制，很專注。不是我印像中的「運動」。沒有音樂，沒有鏡子，沒有任何「表演」的成分。只是一個身體，在一個安靜的房間裡，與自己相處。

Her movements were slow, controlled, focused. Not what I imagined "exercise" to be. No music, no mirrors, no "performance" element. Just a body, in a quiet room, being with itself.

那個下午，我沒有動。我只是看。但離開的時候，我有一種很奇怪的感覺——像是身體被什麼觸碰到了，卻不是通過碰觸的方式。

That afternoon, I didn't move. I just watched. But when I left, I had a strange sensation—as if my body had been touched, but not through touch.

一個問題，追了十八個月

A Question Pursued for Eighteen Months

接下來的幾個月，我開始到處試課。瑜伽、普拉提、芭桿、筋膜放松。我不是為了減肥，不是為了有好身材。我只是想知道：那天我感受到的那個，到底是什麼。

Over the following months, I started trying classes everywhere. Yoga, Pilates, Barre, fascia release. I wasn't doing it to lose weight or to get in shape. I just wanted to understand: what was that thing I felt that day?

問題是：每一種運動，都給了我不同的答案。瑜伽说是呼吸。普拉提說是核心。芭桿說是線條。每一種都很有道理，但每一種都只是部分答案。

The problem: every type of exercise gave me a different answer. Yoga said it was breath. Pilates said it was the core. Barre said it was alignment. Each made sense, but each was only a partial answer.

我在這個問題裡卡了十八個月。一直到有一天，我意識到：也許我問錯了問題。

I was stuck on this question for eighteen months. Until one day, I realized: maybe I was asking the wrong question.

材料很重要

Material Matters

真正的觸發點，是一個非常小的觀察：我在墊子上練習的時候，襪子一直滑。往前滑，往後滑，左右的滑。我需要不斷地停下來調整襪子。

The real trigger was a very small observation: while practicing on the mat, my socks kept slipping. Forward, backward, side to side. I needed to constantly stop to adjust them.

這個觀察看起來不重要，但它困擾了我很久。為什麼沒有人解決這個問題？瑜伽墊有防滑表面，但墊上的襪子沒有對應的解決方案。市場上的「防滑襪」看起來都很運動風、很夸張、很「功能性」——與那個安靜的練習空間格格不入。

This observation seemed insignificant, but it bothered me for a long time. Why hadn't anyone solved this problem? Yoga mats have non-slip surfaces, but socks on mats didn't have a corresponding solution. The "non-slip socks" on the market all looked sporty, flashy, "functional"—out of place in that quiet practice space.

所以我們自己來。

So we decided to do it ourselves.

四十二次測試

Forty-Two Tests

從概念到成品，我們經歷了四十二次材料測試。不是四次，不是十二次，是四十二次。

From concept to product, we went through forty-two material tests. Not four, not twelve—forty-two.

第一次測試用的材料，是家裡找到的一雙普通的瑜伽襪。惨不忍睹。第二次換了一種市面上所謂的「防滑」材料，效果稍好，但摩擦力太強，做完一小時的練習，腳底板都是痛的。第三次、第四次……

The first test used ordinary yoga socks we found at home. Disastrous. The second time we tried a commercially available "anti-slip" material—better but the friction was too strong, and after an hour of practice, our soles ached. Third time, fourth time...

一直到第二十三次，我們才找到一個可以進入小規模生產測試的材料組合。但即使到了這一步，我們仍然發現：每次洗滌之後，性能都會下降。我們需要一種方法，在反覆洗滌後依然保持同樣的防滑效果。

It wasn't until the twenty-third attempt that we found a material combination viable for small-scale production testing. But even at this stage, we discovered: performance degraded after each wash. We needed a way to maintain the same anti-slip effect even after repeated washing.

第三十七次測試的時候，我們終於找到了一個添加劑，可以讓材料在洗滌後反而變得更穩定。第四十二次測試的時候，我們終於確定了最終的配方。

At the thirty-seventh test, we finally found an additive that made the material more stable after washing. At the forty-second test, we finally confirmed the final formula.

為什麼我們存在

Why We Exist

reform. 不是一個「運動品牌」。我們不是在賣襪子。

reform. is not a "sports brand." We're not selling socks.

我們在解決一個很小的問題：當你在墊子上練習的時候，你的襪子不應該分散你的注意力。它應該安靜地在那裡，讓你的身體專注在真正重要的事情上。

We're solving a very small problem: when you're practicing on the mat, your socks shouldn't distract you. They should quietly stay in place, allowing your body to focus on what truly matters.

這個問題很小。小到大多數人會說「就這樣啦，忍一忍就過了」。但我們不認為這是忍一忍的問題。我們認為，這是一個被忽視的機會——一個可以讓墊上的體驗完全不同的起點。

This problem is small. Small enough that most people would say "just deal with it, it passes." But we don't think this is about "dealing with it." We think it's a neglected opportunity—a starting point that could make the mat experience completely different.

所以如果你問我們是誰，我們會說：我們是墊子上那一小時的安静。

So if you ask who we are, we'll say: we're the quiet of that one hour on the mat.
