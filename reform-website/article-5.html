Philosophy 2026.02.10

為什麼我們只做一個產品線

Why We Make Only One Product Line

專注不是限制，是選擇把一件事做到極致。

Focus is not a limitation — it is the choice to perfect one thing.

為什麼可以更多，但選擇更少

Why We Could Do More, But Choose Less

市場上有很多「運動襪」。功能性運動襪壓縮襪、恢復襪、棉質休閒襪。每一種都有它的用途，每一種都有它的受眾。我們完全可以做三種、做出十種、做出三十種。

There are many "sports socks" on the market. Functional sports socks, compression socks, recovery socks, cotton casual socks. Each has its use, each has its audience. We could easily make three, ten, thirty kinds.

但我們選擇只做一種。

But we choose to make only one.

專注的意義：不只是做更少，是關心每個決定

The Meaning of Focus: Not Just Doing Less, but Caring About Every Decision

選擇做更少，不是因為我們没有能力做更多。是因為我們相信：專注是對產品負責的唯一方式。

Choosing to do less isn't because we're incapable of doing more. It's because we believe: focus is the only way to be responsible for a product.

當你把所有的時間、所有的資源、所有的注意力都放在一個東西上的時候，那個東西的品質會到達一個完全不同的水平。當你的工程師只有一個產品需要優化，當你的設計師只有一個形狀需要打磨，當你的供應商只需要理解一個產品的每一個細節——結果是不同的。

When you put all your time, all your resources, all your attention into one thing, that thing's quality reaches a completely different level. When your engineers only have one product to optimize, when your designers only have one shape to refine, when your suppliers only need to understand every detail of one product—the result is different.

這不是犧牲。這是策略。

This isn't sacrifice. It's strategy.

深度的代價

The Cost of Depth

但專注是有代價的。代價是放棄。

But focus has a cost. The cost is giving up.

放棄「可能對某些人有用」的產品。放棄「可以賣得更好」的款式。放棄「這個市場很大」的機會。每一個放棄都是一個決定，每一個決定都會被質疑。

Giving up products that "might work for some people." Giving up styles that "could sell better." Giving up opportunities where "the market is huge." Every giving up is a decision, every decision gets questioned.

但正是這些放棄，定義了我們是誰。

But it's these give-ups that define who we are.

我們對你的承諾

Our Promise to You

當你買了一雙 reform.，你不只是在買一雙襪子。你是在買一個團隊對一雙襪子的全部理解。

When you buy a pair of reform. socks, you're not just buying socks. You're buying a team's complete understanding of one sock.

我們對這雙襪子負責。不是對「運動襪這個品類」負責，不是對「墊上穿著這個場景」負責。僅僅是對這雙襪子負責。

We're responsible for this pair of socks. Not for "the sports socks category," not for "mat wear scenarios." Only for this pair of socks.

如果這雙襪子有任何問題，你找我們。如果這雙襪子可以更好，你告訴我們。如果這雙襪子改變了你對墊上運動的看法，我們和你一起慶祝。

If this pair of socks has any problem, you come to us. If this pair of socks can be better, you tell us. If this pair of socks changed how you think about mat exercise, we celebrate with you.

少，但更好

Less, But Better

這是 reform. 的核心原則。不是「時尚」，不是「身份」，不是「價值觀」。只是一個簡單的信念：當你把一件事真正做好，做對社會有意義的事，商業上的成功會跟著來。

This is reform.'s core principle. Not "fashion," not "identity," not "values." Just a simple belief: when you truly do one thing well, when you do something meaningful for society, business success follows.

所以是的，我們只做一種襪子。因為一種襪子可以做得很好。一种襪子可以成為那個類別的標準。一種襪子可以改變一個人對墊上運動的體驗。

So yes, we make only one kind of sock. Because one kind of sock can be done very well. One kind of sock can become the standard for that category. One kind of sock can change one person's experience of mat exercise.

而當那個人把這個體驗分享給另一個人，我們就完成了使命。

And when that person shares this experience with another, we've completed our mission.
