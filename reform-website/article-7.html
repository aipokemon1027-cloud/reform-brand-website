2026 春季色彩趨勢：柔霧粉與中性灰

Spring 2026 Color Trends: Muted Pink & Neutral Gray

新一季的色彩，從實驗室到伸展墊。

This season's colors, from the lab to the mat.

色彩是一種訊息

Color Is a Message

每一季的色彩趨勢背後，都不是偶然。它們是社會情緒的視覺翻譯。

Behind every season's color trends is no accident. They're visual translations of social mood.

2020 年之前，趨勢是「大膽」。亮黃、電光藍、漸進紫。每一個品牌都在說「突出來」、「被看見」、「站出來」。那是經濟最蓬勃的時代，人們對關注的渴望也最直接。

Before 2020, the trend was "bold." Bright yellow, electric blue, gradient purple. Every brand was saying "stand out," "be seen," "step forward." That was the era of peak economic optimism, and people's desire for attention was most direct.

然後疫情來了。然後一切安靜下來。

Then the pandemic came. Then everything quieted down.

為什麼這個時候安靜的顏色開始流行

Why Quiet Colors Start Trending Now

2023 年，我們觀察到一個反轉。越來越多的品牌開始推出「安靜」的系列。不是「沒有顏色」，是「安靜的顏色」——柔霧粉、暖灰、米白。

In 2023, we observed a reversal. More and more brands started launching "quiet" collections. Not "no color," but "quiet colors"—muted pink, warm gray, cream white.

這不是巧合。這是集體焦慮的視覺表達。

This isn't coincidence. This is visual expression of collective anxiety.

當世界充满不確定性，當「未來」變成一個讓人焦慮的詞，人們傾向於收斂、回歸、安全感。色彩也是一樣：當世界看起來太吵，安静的顏色就開始讓人感到安慰。

When the world is full of uncertainty, when "the future" becomes an anxiety-inducing word, people tend to收敛, return, seek safety. Color is the same: when the world looks too noisy, quiet colors start to feel comforting.

柔霧粉：新的中性色

Muted Pink: The New Neutral

柔霧粉在這個脈絡下特別有意義。傳統上，粉色是一種「女性化」的顏色——它需要被「配得上」，需要被「證明」。但柔霧粉不是傳統的粉色。它更像是「粉色與灰色的愛情結晶」——有彩度的情緒，但沒有攻擊性。

Muted pink is especially meaningful in this context. Traditionally, pink is a "feminine" color—one that needs to be "deserved," needs to be "proven." But muted pink isn't traditional pink. It's more like "pink and gray's love child"—colored emotion without aggression.

這種沒有攻擊性的色彩語言，正是這個時代需要的。

This non-aggressive color language is exactly what this era needs.

暖灰：永恆的基礎

Warm Gray: An Eternal Foundation

暖灰是另一個離散的選擇。在一個「每年公佈一次流行色」的時代，暖灰是少數不會過時的顏色。不是因為它無聊——是因為它幾乎可以與任何東西搭配。

Warm gray is another understated choice. In an era when "a trending color is announced each year," warm gray is one of the few colors that never goes out of style. Not because it's boring—because it can pair with almost anything.

這種靈活性，在一個消費決策越來越困難的時代，是一種美德。

This flexibility, in an era when consumption decisions are increasingly difficult, is a virtue.

調色盤在墊上的應用

The Palette in Practice

在 reform.，我們選擇這兩個顏色，不是因為「它們是趨勢」。是因為它們代表了我們對這個時代的理解：人們需要的不是更多的色彩，是更安全的色彩。

At reform., we chose these two colors, not because "they're trending." It's because they represent our understanding of this era: people don't need more colors, they need safer colors.

安全的色彩不是「無聊的」。安全的色彩是「可以不焦慮地使用」的。當你拿起一雙柔霧粉的襪子，你不需要想「這個場合合適嗎」、「這個天氣合適嗎」、「這個情緒合適嗎」。它們都合適。

Safe colors aren't "boring." Safe colors are "colors you can use without anxiety." When you pick up a pair of muted pink socks, you don't need to think "is this occasion appropriate," "is this weather appropriate," "is this mood appropriate." They're all appropriate.

我們怎麼應用

How We Applied It

所以 reform. 的 2026 春季系列，只有兩個顏色。柔霧粉和暖灰。不是因為我們覺得顏色不重要——是因為我們想把「選擇」的認知負擔降到最低。

So reform.'s Spring 2026 collection has only two colors. Muted pink and warm gray. Not because we think color doesn't matter—it's because we want to minimize the cognitive burden of "choosing."

如果你想要一個可以適用於任何場合、不會錯、不需要想的選擇，這就是我們的答案。

If you want a choice that works for any occasion, can't be wrong, doesn't require thinking—this is our answer.
