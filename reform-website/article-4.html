五個動作，開啟你的每日晨間運動

Five Moves to Start Your Daily Morning Practice

不需要墊子，不需要裝備，只需要一個決定的開始。

No mat. No equipment. Just one decision to begin.

為什麼不需要名字

Why It Doesn't Need a Name

這五個動作之所以存在，不是因為我們覺得「需要一套動作」。是因為在過去三年的觀察和訪談中，有五個動作被反复提到——不是作為「教學內容」，而是作為「困擾」或「突破」。

These five movements exist not because we thought "we need a set of movements." It's because over three years of observation and interviews, five movements were repeatedly mentioned—not as "teaching content," but as "frustrations" or "breakthroughs."

一位受訪者說：「貓式是我最害怕的動作。因為我的手腕會痛，但我不知道怎麼調整。」

One interviewee said: "Cat pose is the movement I fear most. Because my wrists hurt, but I don't know how to adjust."

另一位說：「我每次做嬰兒式就睡著。我以為是我有問題，後來才知道是呼吸不對。」

Another said: "I fall asleep every time I do Child's Pose. I thought something was wrong with me, later I learned it was my breathing."

這些「困擾」和「突破」，才是這套序列真正的骨架。不是理論，是真實使用者的回饋。

These "frustrations" and "breakthroughs" are the real skeleton of this sequence. Not theory—the real feedback from actual users.

為什麼是這五個

Why These Five

篩選的標準很簡單：每個動作都必須同時滿足三個條件。

The filtering criteria were simple: each movement had to meet three conditions simultaneously.

第一個條件：功能性的。它必須解決一個真實存在的問題——不是理論上的問題，而是大多數人在墊上都會遇到的問題。

First condition: functional. It had to solve a real problem—not a theoretical one, but one that most people encounter on the mat.

第二個條件：可量化的。我們需要知道「什麼時候算是做對了」。如果一個動作只能靠「感覺」判斷對錯，它就不在我們的清單上。

Second condition: quantifiable. We needed to know "when is it considered correct." If a movement could only be judged by "feeling," it didn't make our list.

第三個條件：安全的。它不能依賴練習者的柔韌性或力量——必須是一個大多數人都能做、都不會受傷的動作。

Third condition: safe. It couldn't rely on the practitioner's flexibility or strength—it had to be a movement most people could do without injury.

三個條件都滿足的，不多。貓式滿足。呼吸滿足。鳥狗式滿足。但大多數「網紅動作」都會在第二或第三個條件上失敗。

Few movements satisfy all three. Cat pose does. Breathing does. Bird dog does. But most "trending movements" fail on the second or third condition.

為什麼是早晨

Why Morning

如果只能選一個時間，我會選早晨。不是因為早晨特別有效——是因為早晨最穩定。

If I could only pick one time, I'd choose morning. Not because morning is particularly effective—it's because morning is most stable.

大多數人的生活在工作日是相對穩定的——起床時間相對固定，出門時間相對固定。但晚上充滿了變數：加班、應酣、社交、情緒。這些都會讓「晚上運動」變成一件需要「額外动力」的事。

Most people's lives on weekdays are relatively stable—wake-up time is relatively fixed, departure time is relatively fixed. But evenings are full of variables: overtime, social drinking, social events, emotions. These turn "evening exercise" into something requiring "extra motivation."

早晨不需要动力。早晨只需要一個簡單的決定：「今天要不要做」。如果答案是「是」，你已經贏了。

Morning doesn't need motivation. Morning only needs one simple decision: "Will I do it today?" If the answer is "yes," you've already won.

為什麼不是墊子上

Why Not on the Mat

這五個動作，嚴格來說，不是墊上運動。它們是墊外的準備工作。

Strictly speaking, these five movements aren't mat exercises. They're pre-mat preparation.

大多數人在墊上遇到的問題，都不是「墊上」的問題——是身體準備不足的問題。手腕僵硬，意味著支撐動作會痛。肩胛無力，意味著鳥狗式會晃。核心沒有動員，意味著所有動作都會用腰補。

Most problems people encounter on the mat aren't "on the mat" problems—they're body preparation problems. Stiff wrists mean支撑 movements hurt. Weak scapula means bird dog wobbles. Core not activated means all movements compensate with the lower back.

如果墊上的問題來自墊外，那解決方案也在墊外。這就是為什麼這五個動作看起來和墊上運動無關——但它們是墊上運動的「地基」。

If problems on the mat come from off the mat, then the solution is also off the mat. That's why these five movements seem unrelated to mat work—but they are the mat work's "foundation."

持續，而不是完美

Consistency, Not Perfection

這套序列不需要做得完美。它需要做得持續。

This sequence doesn't need to be done perfectly. It needs to be done consistently.

任何動作，只要持續做，都會有效。任何動作，只要不做，就無效。沒有捷徑，沒有秘密，只有重複。

Any movement done consistently will be effective. Any movement not done is ineffective. No shortcuts, no secrets—only repetition.

如果只能選一個原則，那就是這個：不要追逐完美。要追逐持續。

If I could only choose one principle, it's this: don't chase perfection. Chase consistency.
